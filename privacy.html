Inkmoss — Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 7, 2026

Inkmoss ("the app"), published by Sycamore Hill Studios, is a local-only app.

  • No data collection. The app does not collect, store, or share any personal data. There is no analytics code and no advertising SDK.
  • Your journal stays on your device — encrypted. Entries are stored in an encrypted database on your phone; the encryption key is protected by your device's hardware keystore. There is no cloud, no sync, and no server: your journal physically cannot leave your device unless you export it.
  • Lock. The PIN and fingerprint lock run entirely on your device. Your PIN is stored only as a salted hash; your biometric data never leaves the Android system and is never visible to the app.
  • Photos. Photos are chosen through Android's own photo picker and copied into the app's private storage. The app has no access to the rest of your gallery.
  • Export. Exports happen only when you choose, to a location you choose, through Android's own file picker.
  • No accounts. The app requires no sign-up.
  • Purchases. Optional tips are processed entirely by Google Play's billing system. The app never sees or stores your payment details. See Google's Privacy Policy.
  • Deleting data. Deleting an entry removes it immediately. Uninstalling the app removes everything.
