Inkmoss — Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 6, 2026

Inkmoss ("the app"), published by Sycamore Hill Studios, is a local-only app.

  • No data collection. The app does not collect, store, or share any personal data. There is no analytics code and no advertising SDK.
  • Your journal stays on your device — encrypted. Entries are stored in an encrypted database on your phone; the encryption key is protected by your device's hardware keystore. There is no cloud, no sync, and no server. Inkmoss never sends your journal anywhere, and the app is excluded from Android's automatic cloud backup. The only copies that leave this phone are files you create yourself with Export or Backup.
  • Lock. The PIN and fingerprint lock run entirely on your device. Your PIN is stored only as a salted hash; your biometric data never leaves the Android system and is never visible to the app.
  • Photos. Photos are chosen through Android's own photo picker and copied into the app's private storage. The app has no access to the rest of your gallery.
  • Export. Exports happen only when you choose, to a location you choose, through Android's own file picker.
  • Backup and restore. Backup files are written only when you ask, to a place you pick with Android's own file picker, and are encrypted with a passphrase you choose. I never see that passphrase and cannot recover it. If you save a backup into a cloud folder, that copy follows that service's terms, not mine.
  • Earlier automatic backups. Versions of Inkmoss before 1.2.0 did not opt out of Android's automatic backup, so app data from those versions may still be held in your own Google Drive backup. Inkmoss 1.2.0 stops any further uploads, but it cannot delete what is already there. You can remove it at Google One → Storage → Backups → this device → App data.
  • No accounts. The app requires no sign-up.
  • Purchases. Optional supporter packs are one-time purchases processed entirely by Google Play's billing system. The app never sees or stores your payment details; Google Play only tells it which pack you own, so it can unlock that pack's colors and art on your device. See Google's Privacy Policy.
  • Deleting data. Deleting an entry removes it immediately. Uninstalling the app removes everything it stored on this phone. Backup or export files you saved elsewhere stay yours to keep or delete; Inkmoss cannot reach them.
